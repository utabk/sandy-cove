Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: Sandy Cove has no accounts, no adverts and no tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your progress is saved on your device and in your own iCloud. Unless you switch it off, the game sends us how each level went, without your name or contact details, so we can fix levels that are too hard or too easy.

Who we are

Sandy Cove is made by an independent developer. For anything about privacy, write to jperth86@gmail.com.

What stays on your device and in your iCloud

Your progress (levels cleared, sea stars, best scores), coins, boosters, daily gift and settings are saved on your device. Progress, coins, boosters and the daily gift are also saved to your own iCloud account through Apple's iCloud, so they come back after a reinstall and follow you to your other devices. That copy belongs to your Apple account. We cannot see or read it.

Level results we receive

To tune the levels, the game sends one small record each time an attempt at a level ends. You can stop this at any time in the game under Settings → Share level results.

What is sentWhy
Level number, whether it was won, lost or left, moves used and moves left, score, sea stars, how far the goal gotTo see which levels are too hard or too easy
Which try at the level it was, whether extra moves or boosters were used, whether adaptive difficulty was onTo understand where players get stuck
The game's build number and the time the record was savedTo tell versions apart and see changes over time

These records are stored with Apple's CloudKit service. Apple attaches to each record an identifier that it creates for your iCloud account in this game only. It is a string of letters and numbers; it does not tell us your name, email address, Apple Account or device, and we cannot use it to find out who you are. We use it only to count how many different players tried a level. We do not combine these records with any other information, and we do not share or sell them.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or billing details. The game is only told which pack was bought so it can add the coins.

What we do not do

No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites, no analytics or advertising software from other companies, and no selling of data.

Children

Sandy Cove does not ask anyone for personal information and has no chat or social features. Purchases go through Apple's usual parental controls, including Ask to Buy.

Keeping and deleting

To delete your saved progress, delete the game and remove its data from iCloud in Settings → your name → iCloud → Storage on your device. Level results carry no name or contact details, so we cannot pick out yours to delete them one by one; switching off Share level results stops any more being sent. If you write to us we will help as far as we can.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask what information is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Write to the address above and we will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
