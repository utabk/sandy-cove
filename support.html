Support

Stuck, or something not working? Write to jperth86@gmail.com and say which device you use and what happened.

How do I play?

Swipe a piece onto its neighbour to line up three or more of a kind. Four in a line makes a wave piece, an L or T shape makes a blast piece, and five in a line makes a pearl. The full rules are in the game under Settings → How to play.

My coins did not arrive after a purchase

Make sure you are online, close the game completely and open it again: a purchase that was interrupted is delivered the next time the game starts. If the coins are still missing, write to us with the date of the purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will my progress follow me to a new phone?

Yes, if both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Progress, coins and boosters are saved to your iCloud and come back when you install the game again.

How do I start again from level 1?

In the game, open Settings → Reset progress. Your coins and boosters are kept.

How do hearts work?

You have up to five hearts. Starting a level needs one. If you win, you keep it. If you run out of moves and give the level up, or leave a level after making a move, that heart is gone. One heart comes back every 30 minutes, also while the game is closed. You can fill them all up at once with coins: tap the heart at the top of the screen.

A level is too hard

If you lose the same level a couple of times, the game goes a little easier on you, as long as Adaptive difficulty is on in Settings. You can also buy five extra moves with coins when you run out, or use a booster. If a level still feels unfair, tell us its number.

How do I stop the game sending level results?

Open Settings in the game and switch off Share level results. See the privacy policy for what is sent.
